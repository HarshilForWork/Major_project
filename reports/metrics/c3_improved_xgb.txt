WHAT DID NOT HOLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - E5: the NOISE weight collapses to b_N +0.00 once history features are
    added (E2ref without history has b_N +0.62). C3 then explains the risk as
    80% proximity + 20% drift and has no noise channel left to attribute with,
    which is why its noise-vs-drift AUROC (0.378) falls BELOW chance. A better
    black box therefore produced a WORSE C3 decomposition, not a better one.
  - E5 C3 fidelity R^2 0.509 is LOWER than the no-history E2ref (0.609): the surrogate tracks the richer black box less well.
  - E5 noise-vs-drift attribution (0.378) does not beat grouped Shapley on the black box (0.486); C3 still buys structure, not accuracy.
  - E6: the NOISE weight collapses to b_N +0.00 once history features are
    added (E2ref without history has b_N +0.62). C3 then explains the risk as
    80% proximity + 20% drift and has no noise channel left to attribute with,
    which is why its noise-vs-drift AUROC (0.358) falls BELOW chance. A better
    black box therefore produced a WORSE C3 decomposition, not a better one.
  - E6 C3 fidelity R^2 0.419 is LOWER than the no-history E2ref (0.609): the surrogate tracks the richer black box less well.
  - E6 noise-vs-drift attribution (0.358) does not beat grouped Shapley on the black box (0.393); C3 still buys structure, not accuracy.
  - Every E5/E6 number uses the true exam at visit t, so it describes the question 'will today's subtype hold?', NOT the deployment setting where the exam is missing.
  - Planted-mechanism verdicts: 1/3 trained, 3/3 synthetic.
  - The history features are NaN at a patient's first visit, so early visits carry less information than the row count suggests.
